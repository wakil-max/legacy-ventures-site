Accelerator

The Legacy Ventures Acceleration Program is a six-month program for founders anywhere in the world who are already operating and preparing to grow. Built around 17 modules, it runs on a monthly rhythm: live sessions where accomplished founders and industry experts teach, and growth partner sessions where each founder's growth is tracked and analysed. Across the six months we open our networks to founders, and selected participants also receive professional one-to-one coaching. Sessions run live online, so founders can take part from any country.

FAQ

Common questions.

Everything else is on the FAQ page.

How long is the Acceleration Program?
Six months, built around 17 modules, with one live expert session and one growth presentation each month.
Who can apply?
Founders whose business is already operating and entering or preparing for a growth phase, from any sector, any size and any country. We assess the founder before the business model.
Is there an application fee?
No. Legacy Ventures does not charge an application fee. Any program terms specific to a cohort are published with that cohort's call for applications.
How much time does it take each month?
A monthly live session, a live online class each week and a weekly growth partnering session, plus the work you carry out inside your own business in between.
Do I have to present my progress?
Yes. Every founder presents their growth at each monthly session. It is what keeps progress measurable and the cohort accountable to one another.
What results have earlier participants seen?
In the sister program in Bangladesh, the first cohort of 21 businesses recorded 2x to 10x growth over six months, with nearly half reaching 6x to 10x. Results from the international cohorts will be published as they complete.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Categories
Acceleration Program8
What is the Legacy Ventures Acceleration Program?
A structured business growth program designed to help existing entrepreneurs strengthen themselves as founders and sustainably grow their businesses. Participants receive live sessions, coaching, mentoring, practical guidance, accountability, networking opportunities, and access to relevant resources and support networks, from any country.
How can we apply?
When applications open, founders apply by completing the official online application form on this website. Submitting an application does not guarantee selection. Shortlisted applicants may be asked to complete additional tasks, assessments or interviews before final selection.
Who is this program designed for?
Founders, co-founders and business owners who:
  • Are already operating an existing business, venture or non-profit
  • Are committed to growing their business
  • Are willing to work on their personal development as founders
  • Are open to feedback, coaching and accountability
  • Want to build an ethical, sustainable and impact-driven business
Participants do not simply attend classes. They work directly on their existing businesses through coaching, mentoring, assessments, practical work, regular follow-ups and structured growth activities. The program is not designed for people who only have a business idea.
Who cannot apply?
The program is not suitable for people who:
  • Have only a business idea but have not yet started operating the business
  • Are looking only for a course, training, certificate or general entrepreneurship education
  • Are not actively involved in the business they are applying with
  • Cannot commit the required time to live sessions, coaching, practical work and follow-ups
  • Are unwilling to receive feedback, remain accountable or implement necessary changes
  • Are primarily seeking funding rather than founder and business development support
  • Provide false, misleading or incomplete information during application or selection
  • Operate a business that is illegal, unethical, harmful, deceptive or inconsistent with the values of Legacy Ventures
Meeting the basic eligibility requirements does not guarantee selection.
What is the main objective of the program?
To develop capable, ethical and resilient founders who can build sustainable businesses that create meaningful value for people and society. The program follows a people-first, business-second approach. Alongside business growth, it focuses on the founder's mindset, values, intention, integrity, leadership, decision-making and sense of responsibility.
When will applications for the next cohort open?
Start and end dates are announced separately for each cohort and country, on this website and on our social channels. Join the waitlist to be told first.
Is there an application fee?
No. There is no application fee. Any terms specific to a cohort are published with that cohort's call for applications.
Does Legacy Ventures take equity in participants' businesses?
No. Legacy Ventures does not take any equity or ownership stake in a participant's business in exchange for a place in the acceleration program.

Our story

Where We Come From

Legacy Ventures grew out of OnnoRokom Uddokta, an accelerator built in Dhaka by OnnoRokom Group, a conglomerate spanning education, technology, electronics, e-commerce and publishing. In a country where over two million young people enter the workforce each year and accelerators remain few and far between, the team set out to build something different: not a conventional incubator, but a long-term partner that stands by founders through their dreams, challenges and potential.

The same gap exists in most emerging markets. Legacy Ventures takes that model to founders everywhere: a global think tank and startup accelerator promoting value-driven entrepreneurship. We don't accept startups by sector. We accept them by the humanity goal they're solving for.

Our mission

Why We Exist

Our mission is to nurture entrepreneurs who combine innovation with wisdom, building ethical, responsible ventures that create real value while strengthening their communities. We give founders the mentorship, training, networks and practical support they need to grow with clarity and purpose, and we stand with them long after demo day.

Ethics first

Growth that harms people, communities or the land is not growth we want to accelerate.

Founder before model

Business models change. We select for the values and resilience of the person who will carry them.

Long-term partner

The cohort ends. The relationship, the network and the accountability do not.
